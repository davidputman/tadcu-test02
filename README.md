# Tadcu — Episode 2: "The Parcel"

An episode of the **Tadcu** animated cartoon series. Tadcu (Welsh for
"Grandpa"), his sheepdog Bryn, the postmistress Nerys, and a parcel
that contains another parcel — right up until it contains tea.

## What's in here

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |

| `script.md` | Full script, shot list and storyboard notes. |

## How to watch

Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), then press
▶ Play. Controls: play/pause, restart, scene skip, click the bar to scrub by
scene, keyboard: space, →, 1–7.

## Story so far

Episode 1 ("The Great Sheep Escape", in `../test01`): Tadcu's sheepdog Bryn
left the gate open, ninety-nine ewes went walkabout, and only a bag of
teisen lap could end the standoff.

Episode 2 begins the next morning, with the gate firmly latched and the
girls, for once, in exactly the right field. So the trouble arrives by post:
a collection notice, one disagreement about kilograms, and a package
addressed to Tadcu from Pontypool.

## New in this episode

- **Nerys** — postmistress of Cwm Twp. Hair bun, round glasses, postal blue
  overalls. Weighs parcels and arguments on the same scales.
- **The parcel** — cardboard, string, a label with Tadcu's name twice, and
  structural ambiguity about its contents.
- **The scales** — needle pinned at thirty, unchanged since 1987.
