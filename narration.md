# Tadcu — Episode 2: "The Parcel"
## Narration (voice-over) script — recording copy

*One narrator, same voice as Episode 1: warm, unhurried, amused — a
grandfather recounting yesterday's disaster as though it were a weather
report. Music bed: soft harp, with a plucked fiddle for the argument.*

Character lines stay as on-screen subtitle bubbles and are shown under each
beat in *italics* for the editor only. **BOLD = narrator, to be recorded.**

---

### ⏱ 0:00 — TITLE CARD (scene 1, 0:00–0:05.5)

**(NARRATOR, cold open over the dawn hills)**
> **Shwmae — back to Cwm Twp, where the gate is now firmly latched.
> Which means, this week, the trouble had to find a new way in.**

---

### ⏱ 0:05.5 — THE NOTICE (scene 2)

*(Tadcu reads the collection slip; the girls dot the right-hand hill.)*

**0:12.5 (after Tadcu's line "…Nerys counts kilograms the way you'd count
trouble.")**
> **One slip of yellow card, and the whole valley is upside down.**

*(Bryn's ear goes up.)*

**0:16.7**
> **Tea first. Drama second. That's the order.**

---

### ⏱ 0:19.5 — THE POST OFFICE (scene 3)

**0:20.5**
> **The Post Office at Cwm Twp: half banking hall, half mild threat.**

*(Tadcu arrives. Nerys in the window with the glasses.)*

**0:31.7 (after “I AM the signature, love.”)**
> **Nerys has known him forty years, and has never once
> conceded a point.**

---

### ⏱ 0:33.5 — THE PARCEL PROBLEM (scene 4)

**0:34.3**
> **The ticket said three kilograms. The scales said thirty.
> The scales, it should be noted, have said thirty since 1987.**

*(The lid comes off. Another parcel inside.)*

**0:43.7 (after “I want it recorded… another parcel.”)**
> **The Boxing Day of parcels keeps on giving.**

**0:47.1 (after “Keep going. Or I start charging you storage.”)**
> **And at the bottom of it all — one small box, hoping
> very much not to be noticed.**

---

### ⏱ 0:49.5 — THERMOS FROM PONTYPOOL (scene 5)

**0:50.3**
> **Down through cardboard, string and postal history —
> to one very small, very final box.**

*(Tadcu opens it. The new thermos. The note keeps the fuel up.)*

**0:58.5**
> **The old thermos, mind, cracked at Christmas —
> taped ever since on the understanding that it would never die.**

*(Nerys pours a proper cup and says nothing.)*

**1:01.9**
> **Some parcels, it turns out, are addressed to the heart.
> This one had two sugars in it.**

---

### ⏱ 1:04.5 — THE WALK HOME (scene 6)

**1:05.7**
> **One thermos for the road — and ninety-nine ewes who could smell,
> at forty yards, that the bag held no sheep food whatsoever.
> They followed anyway.**

**1:16.3**
> **And so the parcel kept its promise: it contained another parcel —
> right up until it contained tea.**

---

### ⏱ 1:19.5 — END CARD (scene 7)

**1:21.7**
> **Nos da, Cwm Twp. And mind your parcels. They say more
> than they let on.**

*MUSIC OUT.*

## Recording notes

- **Runtime:** ~85 seconds; ~9 narration lines, ≈190 words.
- **The rule stands:** narration lands BEFORE its beat then gets out of the
  way; never read over a bubble.
- **The heart of the episode (0:58–1:03)** is the quietest sixty seconds in
  the series so far — drop the tempo, let the kettle do the foley.
- **"Since 1987"** and **"two sugars in it"** are button lines — full stop,
  small pause, no smile from the booth.
- **End card** said dry, already planning Tuesday.
