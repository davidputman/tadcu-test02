# Tadcu — Episode 2: "The Parcel"

*Series: Tadcu · Episode 2 · Production: test02*

---

## Cold open — TITLE CARD (0:00–0:05.5)

Dawn over Cwm Twp again — and this time, the trouble arrives by post.

> **TADCU**
> *in "The Parcel"*
>
> Subtitle: *Y Parsel!*

### SCENE 1 — THE NOTICE (0:05.5–0:19.5)

**EXT. BWTHYN TADCU — MORNING**

The gate is firmly latched (Tadcu learns slowly), the girls are in exactly
the right field, and a small yellow slip is all it takes.

- **NARRATOR:** "The girls, for once, were precisely in the right field.
  So the trouble arrived by post."
- **TADCU** (reading the slip): "A parcel. For ME. From Pontypool.
  Nerys's ticket says three kilograms — Nerys counts kilograms the way
  you'd count trouble."
- **BRYN:** (ear up. Professional interest.)

Beat: cap tips, thermos drains, stick raised — "Chariot first. Tea is
for after."

### SCENE 2 — THE POST OFFICE (0:19.5–0:33.5)

**EXT. SWYDDFA'R POST, CWM TWP — MORNING**

A blue van. A green door. Nerys the postmistress in the window, reading
glasses down at the end of her nose like a verdict.

- **NARRATOR:** "The Post Office at Cwm Twp: half banking hall,
  half mild threat."
- **TADCU:** "Morning, Nerys! I've come for my parcel — the one with
  my name on it TWICE."
- **NERYS:** "Morning, Tadcu. Signature first."
- **TADCU:** "I AM the signature, love."

### SCENE 3 — THE PARCEL PROBLEM (0:33.5–0:49.5)

**INT. BEHIND THE COUNTER**

The parcel sits on the scales. The numbers do not agree with each other.
The parcel is opened for "inspection".

Gags, in order:
1. The ticket says three kilograms. The scales say thirty.
2. Outer lid flies off: the parcel contains **another parcel**.
3. Nerys posts the middle parcel clean over the counter to Tadcu —
   an arc worthy of the county final.
4. Middle lid flies off: empty, except —

- **TADCU:** "I want it recorded that this parcel contains — ANOTHER PARCEL."
- **NERYS:** "And I want it recorded that it contained another parcel
  when it ARRIVED."
- **NARRATOR:** "The Boxing Day of parcels keeps on giving."
- **NERYS:** "Keep going. Or I start charging you storage."

Beat: a very small box peeks out of the box on the floor, hoping
nobody will mention it.

### SCENE 4 — THERMOS FROM PONTYPOOL (0:49.5–1:04.5)

**INT. THE FLOOR OF THE POST OFFICE**

- **NARRATOR:** "Down through cardboard, string and postal history —
  to one very small, very final box."
- **TADCU** (quiet): "A thermos. New. From Eirlys — my sister, Pontypool.
  'Keep the fuel up.'"
- **NERYS** (pouring him a proper cup, saying nothing, then): "Two sugars?
  Or shall I just pass the tin over?"

The old thermos, mind, cracked at Christmas and taped ever since.
Tadcu — who has weathered every gate in the valley without a sniff —
takes something out of his eye.

### SCENE 5 — THE WALK HOME (1:04.5–1:18.5)

**EXT. THE ROAD HOME — EVENING**

One thermos for the road. A parcel under the arm (empty, has itself been
crying). Bryn rides the parcel, regal. The girls follow — they can smell,
at forty yards, that the bag holds no sheep food whatsoever.

- **TADCU:** "It's TEA, girls. TEA. Were any of you raised in a barn?"
- **BRYN:** (rides the parcel. Regal.)
- **NARRATOR:** "And so the parcel kept its promise: it contained another
  parcel — right up until it contained tea."

**THE END.**

> *Next episode: Tadcu takes Bryn to the vet — an appointment neither of
> them will admit to being afraid of. Ymlaen!*

## Storyboard notes

- **Palette** — Same series green/cream/slate/brick; Post Office adds
  postal blue `#35486b`, cardboard `#b98d52`, string `#8a6a30`.
- **New cast** — Nerys, postmistress of Cwm Twp: hair bun, round glasses,
  postal blue overall. Reads by voice, finishes other people's sentences
  and the Post Office's arguments.
- **Props** — The parcel (box + string + label), its nested lids, the
  pinned weighing-scales (the needle has not moved since 1987), the blue
  van, a distant round window with a sheep cameo.
- **Gag physics** — Every nested box flies in a single parabolic arc
  (`midPass` keyframes); lids hop and spin off (`lidFly`).
- **Continuity** — Gate now latched (one eye on the series rule);
  thermos arc pays off Episode 1's quest for the perfect brew.
- **Source of truth** — `index.html`. The pixels in the browser win any
  argument, same as ever.
